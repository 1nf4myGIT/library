Questions, answered.

What does “modded” mean?
The console runs custom firmware, which lets you install homebrew apps, themes, emulators and backup tools. Your console also still runs official games as normal.
Do the consoles come with games?
Yes. Consoles are sold set up and ready, with commercial games. Use the included MicroSD card reader to install more games from our ROM resources.
Can I still go online?
Modded consoles can carry extra risk online, including account bans on Switch. We set up consoles to reduce that risk and explain how to stay safe, but you use online features at your own risk.
Is there a warranty?
Every console includes a short hardware warranty. Ask when you order for the current length and what it covers.
